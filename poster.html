02 / THE PROBLEM GAP CORE TENSION: GOOD INTENT STALLS AT UNCERTAINTY
HIGH WILLINGNESS TO HELP

People genuinely care about stray animals and frequently encounter distress appeals on Instagram and WhatsApp. Supporter empathy and intent to contribute are consistently high across demographic groups.

THE GAP
ACUTE COGNITIVE UNCERTAINTY

Potential supporters cannot verify whether lesser-known shelters are legally genuine, what items are urgently required, or where monetary donations go. Fragmented UPI appeals create scam anxiety.

Original Problem Statement: "Potential supporters who want to help lesser-known dog shelters may struggle to check whether an organisation is trustworthy, understand what it needs most, and see how their support makes a difference. This uncertainty can make it harder for them to decide when, where, and how to help."

04 / FOUR KEY INSIGHTS EVIDENCE-BASED FOUNDATIONS
INSIGHT 01

Trust Before Transaction

Supporters actively search for statutory legitimacy (12A/80G, AWBI registration) before committing funds. Without transparent audit proof, giving drops off drastically.

INSIGHT 02

Clarity Over Volume

More information is not automatically more reassuring. Bombarding users with endless distressed animals creates emotional fatigue; structured, focused appeals build agency.

INSIGHT 03

Specific Needs Feel Actionable

Itemized requisitions (e.g. ₹450 puppy vaccine, ₹1,200 kibble sack) empower modest budgets and let donors feel tangible impact without corpus intimidation.

INSIGHT 04

Familiarity Differs

Comfort with dogs varies widely. Systems must accommodate passionate on-site volunteers as well as cynophobic or elder supporters through zero-contact digital channels.

05 / THE SUPPORTER JOURNEY 5-STAGE ACTION ARCHITECTURE
PHASE 01

DISCOVER

"Who is out there?"

Regional search filters, verified shelter directory, and social media integration.

PHASE 02

VERIFY

"Can I trust this?"

12A/80G Document Vault, AWBI registration, and physical shelter visit checks.

PHASE 03

UNDERSTAND

"What is needed now?"

Itemized supply appeals with capped targets, unit costs, and urgency badges.

PHASE 04

ACT

"What can I do?"

Micro-donations, direct e-commerce supply shipping, and remote volunteering.

PHASE 05

FOLLOW UP

"What happened next?"

Clinical recovery timelines, dated medical updates, and closure notifications.
