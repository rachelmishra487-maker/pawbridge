Brand Identity Guidelines

PawBridge

“Connecting care with action”

Logo Construction & Rationale

The PawBridge symbol unites the architectural curve of a bridge with the soft anatomy of a canine paw pad. It visualizes the direct connection between grassroot shelter caretakers and the resources, verification systems, and adopters they need to save street dogs with dignity.

Grid: 8pt Monoline Arc
Primary Tone: Olive Green (#536747)
Accent: Soft Gold Node (#E5B95C)
OUR CORE PURPOSE

Empowering grassroot rescuers through verified transparency.

PawBridge is built for the unsung animal-welfare trusts, community shelters, and independent rescuers. It transforms chaotic emergency rescues into structured, verified, and community-funded operations—without bureaucratic friction or paternalism.

IDENTITY KEYWORDS
🛡️ Trust & Verification

Statutory credential checks and private document safety create donor certainty.

🌿 Humane Dignity

Respectful rescue documentation celebrating recovery, never exploitative shock-imagery.

🤝 Community Grounded

Built for the real operational needs of urban indie shelters and feeder networks.

⚡ Action-Oriented

Rapid need-matching for food bags, critical antibiotic vials, and emergency surgeries.

02 / BRAND CHARACTER & VISUAL TONE
Tone of Voice & Aesthetics

A personality defined by institutional rigor and quiet empathy.

PawBridge balances the legal credibility needed for NGO compliance with the visceral warmth of animal caregiving. We intentionally steer clear of loud primary colors, cartoonish dog paws, and alarmist shock marketing.

Trustworthy

The Institutional Anchor

We safeguard legal deeds, FCRA/80G documents, and accounting transparency. Our language is calm, legally precise, and reassuring to high-impact donors and government registries.

Sound like: "Verified Trust Deed DL/2018/01923"

Compassionate

The Caregiver's Heart

Every street dog is recognized as a sentient individual with a medical case history, not an anonymous statistic. Rescues highlight survival, healing, and veterinary rehabilitation.

Sound like: "Bruno’s fracture is healing cleanly with daily splint care"

Transparent

The Open Ledger

No hidden admin costs or vague charity buckets. Support appeals feature real-time progress bars: 14/20 bags of food, exact antibiotic vials, and verified hospital receipts.

Sound like: "₹13,500 of ₹18,000 raised for orthopedic surgery"

Community-Led

The Grassroots Bridge

Designed for field rescuers on mobile devices: accessible in low connectivity, clear large tap targets, and collaborative handoffs between ambulance drivers, vets, and foster homes.

Sound like: "Foster volunteer signed up for weekend post-op recovery"
Iconography Style: 1.5–2px uniform monoline strokes, rounded joints, clear optical metaphors, never cartoonish.
Imagery Principles: Natural ambient daylight, authentic shelter settings, dignified animal recovery portraits, candid field work.

06 / SYSTEM GOVERNANCE & TOKENS
Figma-Ready Component Tokens

Design Guidelines & Token Governance

Strict rules to guarantee consistency, accessibility, and elegance when recreating PawBridge mobile interfaces in Figma and production code.

01

Button Hierarchy

  • Primary: Solid Olive Green (#536747), White text, 10px radius, 12px 20px padding.
  • Secondary: Pale Green (#E8EDE3), Dark Green text, subtle hover tint.
  • Outline: 1.5px Olive border, transparent background, for secondary actions like "Download" or "Back".
  • Emergency CTA: Terracotta (#D98B70), used solely for critical medical fund appeals.
02

Input Fields & Forms

  • Border: 1.5px solid Pale Green (#CBD5E1), active focus turns Olive (#536747).
  • Surface: Pure White (#FFFFFF) inside cards for maximum contrast.
  • Labels: DM Sans 12px Semibold placed strictly above input with clear asterisk for required items.
  • Error State: Terracotta border + 11px Inter inline helper message; never use generic browser alerts.
03

Status Badges & Pills

  • Border-Radius: Full pill radius (999px) with 3px 10px compact padding.
  • Verified: Soft Gold (#E5B95C) or Olive tint with checkmark ✓.
  • Pending: Pale Warm Gold with hourglass symbol ⏳.
  • Private Docs: Pale Green with lock icon 🔒 Encrypted.
04

Iconography Standards

  • Grid Bounding Box: 24×24px with 2px internal optical padding.
  • Stroke Weight: 1.75px uniform monoline; rounded stroke-caps & corner joins.
  • Metaphor Rule: Use clean medical, shelter, document, and shield metaphors; strictly avoid cartoon dog bones.
05

8pt Spacing Grid

  • Spatial Scale: 4px (micro) • 8px (inner gap) • 16px (card padding) • 24px (section gap) • 32px (container).
  • Card Radii: 16px on outer mobile cards, 8–10px on inner input blocks and child tiles.
  • Layout Margin: 16–20px screen edge gutters on 390px mobile viewports.
06

Accessibility & Ethics

  • Contrast: Dark Green (#29352B) on Warm Cream achieves 10.2:1 (well above WCAG AAA 7:1).
  • Sensitive Vault: Legal statutory documents (PAN, Deeds) are never publicly crawlable or indexed.
  • Dignity Rule: Photography must emphasize humane recovery and veterinary care; avoid exploitative trauma.
